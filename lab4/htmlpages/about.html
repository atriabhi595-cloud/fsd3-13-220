The Genesis of Aura

Why make another smartphone when we could invent a companion?

In 2022, four visionary designers and quantum physicists gathered in Zurich with an observation: smartphones had turned mundane. Glass slabs with black bezels demanding attention without offering serenity.

We asked what would happen if a phone could communicate visually through quiet light rather than blaring ringtones, using an intelligent backplate made of micro-refractive crystal layers. Four years of micro-engineering gave life to Aura Phone.
